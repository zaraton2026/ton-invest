❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How to deposit?
1. Copy the deposit address
2. Open your TON wallet (Tonkeeper, etc.)
3. Send the amount with your Telegram UID in the comment
4. Go to @zaratonVerifyBot and send /verify to confirm
5. Your balance will be updated after admin verification
How to buy a plan?
1. Deposit TON first
2. Go to Home page
3. Click "Buy" on your desired plan
4. The amount will be deducted from your balance
When can I withdraw?
Withdrawals are only available AFTER your plan period ends. For example, VIP 1 can withdraw after 30 days.
Can I withdraw task rewards?
No. Task rewards (0.01 TON each) are added to your balance but CANNOT be withdrawn. Only profits from investment plans are withdrawable.
How does referral work?
Share your referral link. When someone joins and makes a deposit, you earn 5% of their deposit amount.
